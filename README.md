# QiddasumTrack

A clean, offline-first personal budget and walking tracker designed for privacy and daily productivity.

- **Track Budgets & Expenses**: Log daily expenses with smart recurring auto-suggestions, monitor incomes, and stay on top of personal cashflow.
- **Recurring Expense Reminders**: Never miss rent, gym, bills, or subscription renewals with proactive visual expiration alerts.
- **Visualize Everything**: Clean, responsive monthly & yearly charts for expenses, category breakdowns, and fitness trends.
- **Fitness & Walking Streaks**: Track daily steps, walking distance, walking streaks, time, and pace with Strava-inspired metrics.
- **100% Private & Local**: All data remains securely stored in local SQLite on your device—no signups, no cloud tracking, works completely offline.
- **Complete Data Portability**: One-tap full backup and restore via Excel (`Checkpoint_DD_MM_YYYY.xlsx`).
